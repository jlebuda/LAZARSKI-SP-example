Collecting your piece

Your piece is ready 3–4 weeks after class. It’s the moment you wait for: come, collect, take a look.

We’ll write to you when your piece is ready to collect.

Glaze firing

Glaze firing isn’t part of the class price and you pay it when you collect: [glaze firing price]. Bring a transfer or cash.

Payments

You pay for classes monthly in advance by bank transfer. The details are in the message you received at the start.

Continuing and pausing

At the end of the term we’ll ask what you’d like to work on next. If you want a break, write before the next month begins.

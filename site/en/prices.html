Rules

Missing a class

Let us know beforehand and you can make the class up in another group the same week.

Group workshops

If someone can’t come we move the date for everyone or change the number of people. Tell us a few days ahead.

Vouchers

A voucher is valid for six months from purchase. The recipient chooses their own date.

Collecting your piece

Your piece is ready 3–4 weeks after class. That’s how long firing takes.

Questions we get a lot

Do I need to bring anything?

No. Clay, glazes, tools and an apron are here.

Do I have to commit for long?

No. You pay for a month in advance, with no long commitment.

Do I need any experience?

No. Beginners are the norm here. You don’t need to know anything.

Why does the wait for my piece take 3–4 weeks?

That’s how long firing takes. The kiln is on site, so the wait is shorter than at studios that send work elsewhere.

Do you teach in English?

Yes, as readily as in Polish.

Can I bring someone?

Yes. A workshop is for 1–8 people, and for regular classes just ask about a free place.

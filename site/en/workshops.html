A pottery workshop

For one person, a couple or a group of up to eight. About 3.5 hours, no experience needed.

Come as three or four

A group of friends is a normal booking here. You don’t join somebody else’s class. The room is yours for the workshop.

Birthdays, a bachelorette party, Women’s Day, a catch-up that keeps getting postponed. You don’t need to know anything. We start with clay, not theory.

What happens and what you take home

  • About 3.5 hours. One of us shows the technique, then you make what you like.
  • Everyone makes their own piece. After 3–4 weeks it’s fired and ready to collect.
  • One person can collect the pieces for the whole group.
  • Price per person: [price per person].

Included

  • clay, glazes, engobes and underglazes
  • tools and an apron
  • bisque firing
  • teaching, with a teacher in the room
  • your place at the table

Not included

  • glaze firing: [glaze firing price]

You pay for it when you collect your piece.

What if someone can’t make it

If someone in your group can’t make the agreed day we move the date for everyone or change the number of people. Tell us a few days beforehand.

Ask about a date for your group

Give us two or three dates. We will reply with two or three free ones, so you do not have to restart the negotiation.

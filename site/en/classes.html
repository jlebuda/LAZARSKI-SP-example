Regular classes

Two hours a week that belong to you. For adults and teens from 15, at every level.

Starting from zero? Most people do.

You don’t need to know anything or buy anything. Clay, tools and an apron are here, and beginners are the norm, not the exception.

  • You bring nothing.
  • You pay monthly in advance. You can stop; there’s no long commitment.
  • You work on your own project, not exercises from a syllabus.

How it works

  1. Tell us which group suits youOr call. We’ll answer warmly and unhurried.
  2. You pay for the first monthIn advance, by bank transfer.
  3. You come to your first classThe first ten minutes are awkward. After that they aren’t.
  4. After 3–4 weeks you collect your pieceFired and finished. It’s the best moment.

Can’t make your slot this week?

You can make the class up in another group the same week. Let us know beforehand and we’ll find a place.
